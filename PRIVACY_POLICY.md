# PowerSearch Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## Overview
PowerSearch is a browser extension that searches your open tabs and browser history locally inside your browser. We designed the extension so that tab contents, search queries, and browsing history results stay on your device.

## Data We Access
PowerSearch requests the following browser permissions:
- `tabs` to list open tabs and focus the selected tab.
- `history` to search your browser history when you choose that scope.
- `<all_urls>` host permission to read visible text from open tabs so local full-text search works.
- `storage` to store settings, recent searches, and pinned searches.
- `sidePanel` to support the side panel experience.

## Data That Stays Local
The following data is processed and stored locally in your browser profile and is not sent to us:
- Extracted tab text used for local search.
- Search queries.
- Search results.
- Recent searches and recent clicks.
- Pinned searches.
- Visual preferences and feature settings.

## Third Parties
We do not use analytics, ad trackers, telemetry SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools inside the extension.

## Data Retention
Local extension data remains in your browser profile until you clear it or remove the extension.

## Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always show the updated date at the top of this document.

## Contact
For privacy questions, support requests, or activation issues, please contact us using the support channel listed in the PowerSearch support documentation.
