# Power Search Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## Overview
Power Search is a browser extension that searches your open tabs and browser history locally inside your browser. We designed the extension so that tab contents, search queries, and browsing history results stay on your device.

## Data We Access
Power Search requests the following browser permissions:
- `tabs` to list open tabs and focus the selected tab.
- `history` to search your browser history when you choose that scope.
- `<all_urls>` host permission to read visible text from open tabs so local full-text search works.
- `storage` to store settings, recent searches, pinned searches, and license state.
- `sidePanel` to support the Pro side panel experience.

## Data That Stays Local
The following data is processed and stored locally in your browser profile and is not sent to us:
- Extracted tab text used for local search.
- Search queries.
- Search results.
- Recent searches and recent clicks.
- Pinned searches.
- Visual preferences and feature settings.

## Payments and License Activation
Power Search offers a paid Pro upgrade through LemonSqueezy.

When you buy Pro:
- LemonSqueezy processes your payment and sends you a license key by email.
- When you activate Pro in the extension, the extension sends your license key and a device instance identifier directly to LemonSqueezy's License API to activate or validate your license.
- The extension stores your license key, activation instance ID, activation counts, and last validation timestamp locally in your browser profile.

We do not receive your tab contents or search history as part of payment processing.

## Third Parties
We use LemonSqueezy as our merchant of record and licensing provider for paid features. LemonSqueezy may process payment information, billing information, customer email addresses, and license activation data under its own privacy policy and terms.

We do not use analytics, ad trackers, telemetry SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools inside the extension.

## License Slot Notice
Each Pro license key supports up to 5 browser profile activations. If you uninstall the extension or uninstall your browser without deactivating the device from Settings first, that activation slot may remain consumed.

## Data Retention
Local extension data remains in your browser profile until you clear it, remove the extension, or deactivate your license. LemonSqueezy retains payment and license records according to its own policies.

## Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always show the updated date at the top of this document.

## Contact
For privacy questions, support requests, or activation issues, please contact us using the support channel listed in the Power Search support documentation.
